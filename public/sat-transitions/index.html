SAT TRANSITIONS

Decide what the ideas are doing before you decide which transition belongs between them.

Transitions belong to the Expression of Ideas domain in College Board’s current digital SAT framework. The test is usually less about knowing a long list of transition words and more about identifying the logical relationship between ideas.

A reliable SAT transitions process

  1. Read both sides without the transition. Understand each idea independently.
  2. Name the relationship in plain language. Is the second idea a contrast, result, example, addition, continuation, or sequence?
  3. Predict the transition family. Think “contrast word” before comparing however and nevertheless.
  4. Eliminate choices with the wrong job. A grammatically smooth word can still create the wrong logic.
  5. Reread the completed text. Make sure the transition matches both meaning and punctuation.
Common trap: choosing a familiar transition because it sounds natural without checking the actual relationship between the sentences.

Common logical relationships

Contrast or concession

The second idea differs from, limits, or pushes against the first. Examples can include however, nevertheless, or in contrast depending on the sentence.

Cause or result

The second idea follows from the first. Examples can include therefore, consequently, or as a result.

Addition or reinforcement

The second idea supplies another compatible point. Examples can include furthermore, in addition, or also.

Example or specification

The second idea illustrates or narrows the first. Examples can include for example or specifically.

Sequence

The transition marks order in time, procedure, or argument. Examples can include next, subsequently, or finally.

Do not memorize one word for one relationship

Several transition words can perform similar rhetorical jobs, and individual words can carry slightly different shades of meaning. Strong preparation teaches the relationship category first, then the precision needed to choose among options in context.

SATprep.io approach: practice explanations name the logical relationship and show why the correct transition performs that job. The diagnostic does not teach or reveal answers while measuring the baseline.

Track transitions separately from grammar

Transitions are about relationships between ideas, while Standard English Conventions questions focus on sentence structure, usage, and punctuation. A student who struggles with transitions does not necessarily need a broad grammar review.
