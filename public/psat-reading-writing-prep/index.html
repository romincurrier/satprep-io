PSAT READING & WRITING PREP

Practice the reasoning, language, and editing skills that drive PSAT Reading and Writing.

The PSAT/NMSQT and PSAT 10 Reading and Writing section contains 54 questions in 64 minutes, divided into two 32-minute modules. Questions draw from four domains that also appear across the digital SAT Suite.

The four Reading and Writing domains

Information and Ideas

Central ideas and details, command of evidence, and inferences. Students must identify what a text states, what it supports, and what can logically be concluded.

Craft and Structure

Words in context, text structure and purpose, and cross-text connections. These tasks focus on vocabulary in context and how authors build meaning.

Expression of Ideas

Rhetorical synthesis and transitions. Students choose language or organization that best accomplishes a stated writing goal.

Standard English Conventions

Boundaries plus form, structure, and sense. These questions test sentence structure, usage, and punctuation decisions in context.

Short passages, one question at a time

Current digital SAT Suite Reading and Writing questions use short texts or passage pairs, generally followed by a single multiple-choice question. That format rewards precise reading: the student needs to identify exactly what the question is asking and use only the evidence needed to answer it.

Some Information and Ideas questions also use informational graphics such as tables or graphs, so practice should include integrating quantitative information with text rather than treating reading and data as separate skills.

Practice should teach a repeatable decision process

Evidence questions

Separate what the text actually supports from answers that overstate, generalize, or add information that is not present.

Words in context

Use the role of the word in the sentence and surrounding logic rather than choosing a familiar dictionary meaning automatically.

Transitions

Name the relationship between ideas first—contrast, cause, example, addition, sequence—then select the transition that performs that job.

Grammar and punctuation

Find the sentence core, determine whether clauses are complete, and apply punctuation or agreement rules to that structure.

Assessment first, explanations during learning

SATprep.io keeps the initial diagnostic assessment-only. The student does not receive correct/incorrect feedback while the diagnostic is in progress, because immediate teaching could influence later diagnostic answers. During learning and practice sessions, the student does receive the correct answer and an explanation of the reasoning process after responding.
