What the learning plan uses

Prior testing

Supported score reports can provide useful baseline evidence instead of forcing the system to start from zero.

Diagnostic performance

An assessment-only diagnostic measures current skills without teaching answers during the test.

Practice performance

Correctness, skill mastery, and repeated work help the recommended sequence change over time.

Student goal

The target exam and score help keep the preparation experience focused on the student's objective.

Students learn; parents see the big picture.

Good preparation should not require a parent to inspect every homework problem. SATprep.io is being built so families can see whether sessions are being completed, which skills are improving, where the student is struggling, and whether the learning plan is moving forward.

Privacy-aware account setup: student signup begins with age. Children under 13 are directed to parent or guardian setup before a student account is created.

Preparation should answer the question: “What should we work on next?”

A personalized learning plan helps turn broad test-prep goals into specific next steps. Students receive instruction and guided practice; parents receive a clearer view of the preparation process.
