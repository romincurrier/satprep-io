How adaptive SAT preparation works

1. Establish a baseline

Upload prior testing when available and complete a diagnostic that measures performance without revealing answers during the assessment.

2. Learn the process

Instruction focuses on the rule, reasoning process, common traps, and the skill behind the question.

3. Practice with feedback

Practice sessions show whether the response was correct, the correct answer, and an explanation of how to reach it.

4. Keep adapting

Mastery estimates and recommended skills update as new evidence is collected.

Why skill-level preparation matters

A broad section score can tell a student that Math or Reading and Writing needs work, but it does not always show what to study next. SATprep.io is designed to narrow that question to specific skill areas so study time can be directed toward the most useful next step.
