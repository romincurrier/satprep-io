How to choose an SAT test date

Start with your real calendar

Mark school exams, sports, travel, performances, application deadlines, and other high-demand weeks. A theoretically ideal date is not useful if the preparation period is unrealistic.

Leave room for more than one attempt if appropriate

Some students prefer a schedule that leaves another administration available later. That can reduce pressure on any single test date without assuming that every student needs multiple attempts.

Use a baseline before setting the workload

A student with a narrow set of skill gaps needs a different plan from a student who needs foundational instruction across several domains.

Protect the final weeks

The last part of a plan should include realistic practice under test conditions, error review, and schedule stability rather than a sudden increase in study volume.

What about SAT School Day?

For the 2026–27 school year, College Board lists a fall in-school testing window from October 1–30, 2026 for SAT School Day, PSAT/NMSQT, and PSAT 8/9. The spring 2027 window for SAT School Day and PSAT 10 is March 1–April 30, 2027. Schools and districts manage registration for in-school administrations.

If your school offers SAT School Day, include that date in your planning before deciding whether a weekend administration is also useful.

Build your study timeline backward from test day

A practical plan begins with the test date, then divides the available weeks into four jobs: identify the baseline, teach weak skills, practice and reinforce those skills, and finally rehearse under realistic timing. The amount of time in each phase should change with the student's evidence.
