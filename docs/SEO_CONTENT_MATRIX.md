# SATprep.io SEO Content Matrix

Status: current implementation map plus prioritized backlog. Search-volume numbers are intentionally omitted until Search Console / keyword-platform data is available; do not invent volume estimates.

## SEO strategy
SATprep.io should earn search visibility by publishing useful, accurate SAT/PSAT preparation information that naturally demonstrates the product's skill-based approach. Content should answer the user's question first, use current official test facts, avoid score guarantees, and direct readers to a relevant next step.

Core topical authority pillars:
1. SAT preparation planning.
2. SAT Math skills.
3. SAT Reading and Writing skills.
4. PSAT/NMSQT preparation and SAT-suite continuity.
5. Parent guidance and progress visibility.
6. Adaptive learning / diagnostics / study methodology.

## Required methodology/trust pages before commercial launch
These are product trust pages, not just SEO content:
- `/how-it-works/` — evidence sources, diagnostic, learning loop, mastery updates.
- `/content-quality/` — original-question policy, editorial review, independent QA, no copied College Board bank.
- `/security-privacy/` — high-level trust summary after legal/privacy review; not a substitute for Privacy Policy.
- `/faq/` — billing, diagnostic, prior-score upload, parent access, supported exams, cancellation.
- `/contact/` or `/support/` — real support route before public customers.

## On-page standards
Every indexable page should include:
- Unique descriptive `<title>`.
- Unique meta description written for the actual page intent.
- Canonical URL.
- One clear H1.
- Helpful body copy that fully answers the query rather than thin keyword text.
- Relevant internal links to parent/child pages and one product CTA.
- Current non-affiliation language when discussing SAT/PSAT/College Board.
- Structured data only when it accurately describes visible content.
- Accessible headings, links, and mobile layout.
- No fabricated testimonials, outcome statistics, rankings, or score guarantees.

## Internal-linking model
- Homepage links to SAT, PSAT, Parent, and methodology pages.
- SAT pillar links to Math, Reading & Writing, Study Plan, SAT-vs-PSAT.
- Math pillar links to each Math skill cluster and Study Plan.
- Reading & Writing pillar links to each RW skill cluster and Study Plan.
- PSAT pillar links to SAT-vs-PSAT, PSAT study plan, and relevant shared skill pages.
- Every leaf skill page links upward to its pillar and laterally to 2–4 closely related pages.
- Parent pages link to product methodology/pricing rather than forcing parents through student-oriented skill pages.

## Content QA rules
- Test facts should be checked against current official College Board material before publishing and rechecked at least annually or when College Board announces changes.
- National Merit rules should be checked against National Merit Scholarship Corporation and College Board; never imply that a particular score guarantees recognition or scholarship.
- Use original examples. Do not reproduce official question-bank items unless a license clearly permits the intended use.
- If an article includes a worked SATprep.io practice item, use an item approved for public instructional use—not a live diagnostic item.
- Do not expose production diagnostic answer keys in crawlable content.

## Measurement plan once approved
Evaluate content by qualified behavior, not pageviews alone:
1. Search impressions and click-through by query/page.
2. Engaged landing sessions.
3. Product CTA clicks.
4. Signup starts.
5. Diagnostic starts/completions by landing page at an aggregate level approved by privacy review.
6. First learning-session completion.
7. Paid conversion and retention only after lawful attribution is implemented.

Search data should determine the next editorial expansion; do not manufacture keyword-volume estimates before real data exists.
